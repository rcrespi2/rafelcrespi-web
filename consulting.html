AI, Robotics & Employment in the Balearic Islands

Currently in development · commissioned by CES Illes Balears (Consell Econòmic i Social) and the Govern de les Illes Balears, managed via FUEIB, with UIB's Department d'Economia de l'Empresa

A prospective study — Rafel as study director — on how AI, robotics, and automation will reshape occupations and population dynamics in a tourism-dominated regional economy. Combines a structural analysis of the region's occupation/population relationship, an international literature review on task-level automation exposure (with contributions from Juan Francisco Jimeno, Banco de España/Consejo de la Productividad de España; Luis Garicano), an expert-based vulnerability assessment for tourism-sector occupations (with Turistec), a firm-productivity analysis, and forward-looking scenarios to inform public policy on services, infrastructure, and workforce training.

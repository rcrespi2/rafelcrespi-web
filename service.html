Academic & Professional Service

Editorial roles, peer review, and service to the research system — distinct from the applied consulting work on the Consulting page.

Committees & boards

  • Doctoral Program Coordinator, Universitat de les Illes Balears — since 22 Sept 2014 (current). Coordinates UIB's side of the joint doctoral program with UAB and UPNA (the DEMO program) — the reason for his recurring discussant/chair role at the annual DEMO Workshops in Barcelona.
  • Program/Organizing Committee, Workshop on Corporate Governance and Investment (WCGI) — standing member alongside Marc Goergen (IE University), Niels Hermes (University of Groningen), Miguel A. García Cestona (UAB), and Marc Steffen Rapp (Philipps-Universität Marburg). WCGI is a peer workshop series running since 1998, rotating annually across European universities (Copenhagen 2022, Marburg 2023, Aberdeen 2024, Vaasa 2025, Vilnius 2026).
  • Jury member, Premio de Investigación y Estudio Antonio Dionis Soler — research award, Instituto de Analistas Financieros / Fundación de Estudios Financieros. Near-continuous service since 2008 (2008–2010, 2015–2025). Award page
  • Recurring external evaluator, Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (UC3M), 2021–2026 — faculty search committees (including a 2026 Chair search), postdoctoral-program and "recualificación" (promotion) evaluation, doctoral thesis panels, and the Consejo Social excellence awards committee.
